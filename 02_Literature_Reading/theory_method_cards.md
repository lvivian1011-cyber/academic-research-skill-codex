> Can this theory or method become part of my PhD proposal, literature review, or supervisor communication?

# Initial Card List

## Participatory Design

### 1. Short definition

Participatory design involves users and stakeholders as active contributors in the design process rather than treating them only as research subjects or end users.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for framing older adults, younger generations, caregivers, community workers, and service providers as co-creators of community-based services.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the methodology section and justify why the research will involve community stakeholders in defining problems, generating concepts, testing prototypes, and reflecting on social acceptability.

## Co-design

### 1. Short definition

Co-design refers to collaborative design processes in which researchers, designers, users, and other stakeholders jointly explore problems and develop solutions.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for designing intergenerational digital services with older adults and community members rather than designing for them from a top-down perspective.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can be used to explain workshops, prototyping sessions, stakeholder mapping, service journey mapping, and iterative evaluation.

## Design Anthropology

### 1. Short definition

Design anthropology combines ethnographic inquiry with design practice to understand everyday life, situated practices, future possibilities, and socio-material relations.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for connecting community life, ageing experience, intergenerational interaction, and digital service design through situated fieldwork.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support an ethnographic and context-sensitive approach to studying community ageing and digital service practices.

## Service Design

### 1. Short definition

Service design focuses on the design of interactions, touchpoints, processes, stakeholder relationships, and service systems.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for framing intergenerational digital community services as a service ecosystem rather than a single app, product, or interface.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support service journey mapping, stakeholder mapping, service blueprinting, and community service prototyping.

## Service Ecosystem

### 1. Short definition

A service ecosystem frames services as networks of actors, resources, practices, institutions, and value co-creation processes.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for analyzing how older adults, families, children, community organizations, digital platforms, volunteers, and public services interact in community settings.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the theoretical framework and expected contribution around systemic service design.

## Age-friendly Community

### 1. Short definition

An age-friendly community supports older adults' participation, mobility, social connection, access to services, and wellbeing across everyday environments.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Directly relevant to the ageing and community dimension of the project.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the background, research gap, and contribution sections.

## Intergenerational Practice

### 1. Short definition

Intergenerational practice creates opportunities for meaningful contact, learning, support, and collaboration between people of different generations.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Central to the user's emerging interest in intergenerational digital community services and shared community wellbeing.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the core research problem, case selection, and design intervention logic.

## Social Infrastructure

### 1. Short definition

Social infrastructure refers to the physical, organizational, and social spaces that enable people to meet, interact, build trust, and participate in collective life.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for linking community spaces, service systems, and intergenerational interaction.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can help frame the project beyond digital technology by emphasizing place-based community relations.

## Responsible AI

### 1. Short definition

Responsible AI refers to the ethical, inclusive, transparent, accountable, and socially acceptable development and use of AI systems.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful if the project includes AI-supported community services, recommendation systems, care coordination, or digital participation tools.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the digital ethics and social acceptability dimension of the proposal.

## Situated Prototyping

### 1. Short definition

Situated prototyping tests design ideas in real or realistic contexts, allowing researchers to understand how prototypes interact with everyday practices, places, and stakeholder relationships.

### 2. Relevance to my PhD

Useful for connecting design experimentation with actual community life and ageing-related service contexts.

### 3. Possible proposal use

Can support the research-through-design and evaluation sections.
