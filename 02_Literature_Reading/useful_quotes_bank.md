# Useful Quotes and Paraphrases Bank

Use this file to collect short, strategically useful quotations and paraphrases from papers.

The goal is not to accumulate long quotations. The goal is to convert literature into usable language for:

- research proposal
- literature review
- supervisor email
- motivation letter
- personal statement
- research identity statement

## Rules

1. Always record the source clearly.
2. Always add your own paraphrase.
3. Always tag where the quote can be used.
4. Avoid over-quoting. Prefer paraphrase and synthesis.
5. Keep direct quotations short and precise.
