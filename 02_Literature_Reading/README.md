# 02｜Literature Reading

This folder is the literature-reading engine for the project **PhD Application 2026 – Design, Ageing & Community**.

Its purpose is not only to summarize papers, but to convert every reading into usable PhD application material: research positioning, proposal arguments, supervisor emails, literature review clusters, theoretical framing, and method choices.

## Core principle

Every paper should answer this question:

> How can this paper help me develop, justify, or communicate my PhD direction around intergenerational digital community services, ageing, participatory design, design anthropology, responsible AI, inclusive services, and community-based wellbeing?

## Folder structure

- `paper_reading_index.md` — master index of all read and unread papers.
- `paper_note_template.md` — reusable template for structured paper notes.
- `phd_relevance_matrix.md` — scoring matrix for judging PhD relevance.
- `theory_method_cards.md` — reusable cards for theories, concepts, and methods.
- `literature_clusters.md` — thematic clustering of papers.
- `useful_quotes_bank.md` — quotation and paraphrase bank for proposal, PS, and supervisor emails.
- `reading_workflow.md` — step-by-step workflow for reading and converting papers.

## Recommended reading output

Each paper note should include:

1. Basic bibliographic information.
2. Research problem and context.
3. Theoretical framework.
4. Methodology.
5. Key findings.
6. Limitations and research gap.
7. Relevance to my PhD direction.
8. How the paper can be used in proposal, literature review, motivation letter, personal statement, and supervisor email.
9. Critical reflection.
10. Next reading directions.

## Connection with other project modules

This folder directly supports:

- `01_Research_Direction_Map` — strengthens research identity, research questions, keywords, and theoretical framing.
- `03_Supervisor_Matching` — helps assess fit with supervisors and research groups.
- `04_Proposal_Development` — provides literature review, research gap, theory, and methodology material.
- `05_Motivation_Letters_Emails` — provides supervisor-specific references and intellectual hooks.
- `06_Application_Tracker` — helps track which literature supports which application.
