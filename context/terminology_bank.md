# Terminology Bank

- intergenerational digital community services
- ageing and community wellbeing
- age-friendly communities
- inclusive service design
- participatory design
- co-design

- ethnographic inquiry
- situated prototyping
- responsible AI
- human-centred AI
- community-based services
- social innovation
- service ecosystems
- stakeholder engagement
- care infrastructures

## Preferred Research Direction Phrases

- My emerging PhD interests lie at the intersection of service design, ageing, participatory design, and responsible digital technology.
- I am interested in how community-based digital services can support more inclusive intergenerational relationships.
- My previous teaching and interdisciplinary project experience has led me to consider how design research can respond to ageing, care, and community wellbeing.
- I hope to further develop this direction through ethnographic inquiry, co-design, and situated prototyping.

## Cautious Phrases

Use these when the research direction should remain open:

- tentative direction
- emerging research interest
- possible PhD focus
- I am interested in exploring
- I hope to investigate
- this may provide a useful direction for future research

## Phrases to Avoid

Avoid language that sounds too fixed or exaggerated:

- I will definitely prove that...
- My research has already solved...
- This is the perfect match for my research.
- I am certain that my PhD topic will be...
- I have extensive expertise in AI unless supported by clear evidence.
