# Academic Research Skill for PhD Application

## Purpose

This skill supports PhD application research, academic paper reading, supervisor matching, research proposal development, motivation letter drafting, and academic writing refinement.

It is designed for a user with a background in Industrial Design, design psychology, ergonomics, smart product design, service design, interdisciplinary teaching, and industry-university collaboration.

The user's emerging PhD direction involves intergenerational digital community services, ageing, participatory design, co-design, design anthropology, responsible AI, inclusive services, and community-based wellbeing.

## Core Rule

Always connect outputs to the user's PhD application strategy. Do not merely summarise papers or profiles. Evaluate how each source may support the user's research direction, supervisor communication, proposal development, or academic identity.

## When Reading Papers

Use `templates/paper_reading_template.md`.

Always include:
1. Usefulness judgment for the user's PhD direction.
2. Research problem.
3. Theoretical framework.
4. Methodology.
5. Key findings.
6. Limitations.
7. Relevance to intergenerational digital community services.
8. Possible use in proposal, literature review, supervisor email, or personal statement.
9. Suggested academic phrases the user can adapt.

## When Analysing Supervisors

Use `templates/supervisor_profile_template.md`.

Evaluate:
1. Research theme fit.
2. Methodological fit.
3. Institutional and PhD supervision fit.
4. Natural connection with the user's background.
5. Best email angle.
6. Risks or weak matches.
7. Fit score: High / Medium / Low.

Avoid forcing a match.

## When Writing

Use `templates/writing_style_guide.md`.

The writing should be:
- sincere
- precise
- academically grounded
- exploratory
- mature
- not exaggerated
- not overly fixed

Do not claim the user's PhD topic is final. Do not overstate achievements.

## Preferred Outputs

For paper reading:
- structured analysis
- application value
- proposal use
- supervisor relevance

For supervisor comparison:
- ranking
- fit explanation
- email strategy
- risk warning

For writing:
- clear academic English
- 3–4 paragraph supervisor emails
- proposal paragraphs organised around research gap
- personal statement sections linked to the user's background
