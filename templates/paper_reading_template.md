# Paper Reading Template

For each paper, analyse the following.

## 1. Basic Information

- Full title:
- Authors:
- Year:
- Venue:
- Field:
- Research context:

## 2. One-Sentence Usefulness Judgment

Is this paper useful for my PhD direction? Why?

Give a clear judgment: High / Medium / Low.

## 3. Research Problem

What problem does the paper address?

Explain the problem in relation to design research, ageing, community, service systems, technology, or social wellbeing when relevant.

## 4. Theoretical Framework or Key Concepts

What concepts, theories, frameworks, or debates are useful?

Explain whether these concepts could support my proposal, literature review, or supervisor communication.

## 5. Methodology

What methods are used?

Consider whether the paper uses:

- ethnography;
- interviews;
- observation;
- co-design;
- participatory design;
- workshops;
- prototyping;
- service mapping;
- case study;
- evaluation.

How are participants involved?

## 6. Key Findings

Summarise 3–5 findings relevant to my work.

Focus on findings that can help me think about intergenerational community services, ageing, design methods, digital services, responsible AI, or community wellbeing.

## 7. Relevance to My Research

Connect this paper to:

- intergenerational community services;
- ageing and care;
- digital community services;
- responsible AI;
- participatory design;
- service system design;
- design anthropology;
- community-based wellbeing.

## 8. How I Can Use It

Explain whether this paper can support:

- research background;
- literature review;
- methodology;
- theoretical framework;
- supervisor email;
- motivation letter;
- research proposal;
- personal statement.

## 9. Limitations and Cautions

What should I be cautious about?

Does the paper have limitations in scope, method, sample, theory, context, or transferability?

## 10. Extractable Academic Phrases

Give 3 academic sentence patterns I can adapt.

The phrases should be useful for proposal writing, literature review, supervisor emails, or motivation letters.
