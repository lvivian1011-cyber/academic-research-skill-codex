# Research Proposal Template

## Working Title

Write a tentative title. Keep it focused but not overly fixed.

## 1. Research Background

What social, demographic, technological, or design problem motivates this research?

Consider:

- ageing population;
- community wellbeing;
- intergenerational relations;
- digital exclusion;
- community service systems;
- responsible digital technology;
- limitations of current service design approaches.

## 2. Research Gap

What is missing in existing research?

The gap may relate to:

- insufficient attention to intergenerational community services;
- limited integration of ageing, digital services, and participatory design;
- weak understanding of older adults' situated everyday practices;
- limited discussion of responsible AI in community service contexts;
- lack of design-led, ethnographic, or co-design approaches.

## 3. Research Aim

What does the project aim to explore, understand, design, or evaluate?

Use exploratory language rather than overly fixed claims.

## 6. Methodology

## 7. Expected Contribution

Contribution to:

- design research;
- ageing and community studies;
- participatory service design;
- responsible digital technology;
- community wellbeing;
- PhD supervisor's research area.

## 8. Supervisor Fit

How does this proposal connect to the potential supervisor's work?

Mention specific themes, methods, papers, projects, or research centres when evidence is available.

## 9. Risks and Scope Control

What should be narrowed, avoided, or kept exploratory?

Consider risks such as:

- topic too broad;
- AI component not sufficiently grounded;
- ageing/intergenerational focus not clearly defined;
- method too ambitious;
- weak evidence for supervisor fit.
