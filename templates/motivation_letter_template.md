# Motivation Letter Template

Use this template for PhD inquiry emails, motivation letters, and supervisor-contact writing.

## Paragraph 1: Research Identity

Introduce my background as an Industrial Design lecturer.

Explain briefly how teaching, design psychology, ergonomics, smart product design, interdisciplinary collaboration, or community-oriented projects have led me toward PhD research.

## Paragraph 2: Research Interest

Explain my emerging interest in:

- intergenerational digital community services;
- ageing and community wellbeing;
- participatory design and co-design;
- service design;
- design anthropology;
- responsible digital technology or AI.

Keep the direction open and exploratory.

## Paragraph 3: Supervisor / Programme Fit

Connect my interest to the supervisor's research.

Mention 1–2 specific works, themes, methods, projects, or research groups when evidence is available.

Avoid generic praise.

## Paragraph 4: Future Development

Explain what I hope to develop through PhD study.

The ending may politely ask whether the supervisor would be open to discussing possible PhD supervision or research fit.

## Tone Rules

- sincere;
- specific;
- academically grounded;
- not exaggerated;
- not overly emotional;
- not too fixed;
- not generic.

## Useful Sentence Patterns

- I am writing to ask whether you might be open to discussing a possible PhD research direction related to...
- My previous teaching and interdisciplinary project experience has led me to become increasingly interested in...
- I am particularly drawn to your work on...
- I hope to further develop this direction through doctoral research that combines...
- I would be grateful for the opportunity to ask whether this direction might align with your current supervision interests.
