# Literature Review Matrix

Use this template to transform individual paper notes into a structured literature review.

The goal is not to list papers one by one. The goal is to organise evidence around themes, concepts, methods, gaps, and proposal opportunities.

## 1. Theme

What research theme does this paper belong to?

Possible themes:

- ageing and community wellbeing;
- intergenerational services;
- participatory design and co-design;
- design anthropology;
- responsible AI and digital services;
- inclusive service design;
- community-based care;
- service ecosystems.

## 2. Problem Area

What social, design, technological, or methodological problem does this paper address?

## 3. Key Concepts

List concepts that may support my proposal.

Explain why each concept is useful.

## 4. Methodological Value

What methods can I learn from this paper?

Consider:

- ethnography;
- interviews;
- observation;
- co-design workshops;
- participatory prototyping;
- service mapping;
- stakeholder analysis;
- qualitative analysis;
- evaluation.

## 5. Relation to My Research

How does this paper connect to:

- ageing;
- intergenerational community;
- participatory design;
- service design;
- responsible AI;
- community wellbeing;
- digital service systems?

## 6. Possible Citation Function

This paper can be used to:

- define the problem;
- support the research gap;
- justify the method;
- explain the design context;
- support supervisor fit;
- strengthen motivation letter;
- develop research questions.

## 7. Gap

What does this paper not address?

What limitation creates space for my proposed research?

## 8. My Proposal Opportunity

What research opportunity does this create for me?

Write this as a possible proposal sentence or research gap statement.

## 9. Literature Review Placement

Where could this source fit?

- Background;
- theoretical framework;
- methodology;
- research gap;
- case/context;
- discussion of design contribution.
