# Motivation Letter Template

## Paragraph 1: Research Identity
Introduce my background as an Industrial Design lecturer and explain how my teaching and project experience led me toward PhD research.

## Paragraph 2: Research Interest
Explain my emerging interest in intergenerational digital community services, ageing, participatory design, service design, design anthropology, and responsible technology.

## Paragraph 3: Supervisor / Programme Fit
Connect my interest to the supervisor's research. Mention 1–2 specific works, themes, or methods. Avoid generic praise.

## Paragraph 4: Future Development
Explain what I hope to develop through PhD study. Keep the topic open, mature, and research-oriented.

## Tone Rules
- sincere
- specific
- not exaggerated
- not overly emotional
- not too fixed
- not generic
