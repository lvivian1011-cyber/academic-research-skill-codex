# Supervisor Shortlist

This file tracks priority supervisors for the PhD application project.

Last updated: 2026-09-10

## Priority Scale

- **A**: Strong fit; worth reading papers and preparing a targeted email.
- **A-**: Strong fit but needs clearer vacancy, funding, supervision, or institutional route.
- **B+**: Good secondary fit; useful as a second-wave target, co-supervisor possibility, or vacancy-triggered contact.
- **B**: Potential fit; needs more evidence from publications, projects, or supervision context.
- **C**: Interesting but currently lower strategic priority.

## Current Strategy Notes

For Nordic and Dutch systems, many doctoral routes are vacancy-based or project-funded. Outreach should not ask only for general supervision. A stronger approach is to ask whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities, open calls, scholarship routes, or funded projects connected to the supervisor's current work.

After sending 7 emails with 0 replies, shift to a broader regional pipeline:

1. Build longlists by country and research cluster.
2. Track vacancies/open calls weekly.
3. Send only 3–5 highly tailored emails per wave.
4. For each A / A- supervisor, read 2–3 papers or current project pages before emailing.
5. Avoid emailing multiple people in the same small group at the same time unless the email clearly asks who would be the most appropriate contact.
