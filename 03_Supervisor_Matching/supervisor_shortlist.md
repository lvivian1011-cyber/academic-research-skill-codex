## Priority Scale

## Notes

For KTH and similar Nordic systems, doctoral recruitment is often connected to advertised doctoral positions and funded projects. The outreach strategy should therefore avoid asking only for general supervision. A stronger approach is to ask whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities connected to the supervisor’s current projects, and whether the applicant’s design research background could be relevant.
