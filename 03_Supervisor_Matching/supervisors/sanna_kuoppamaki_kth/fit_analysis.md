## 7. Risks / Weaknesses

### Risk 1｜KTH PhD recruitment may be position-based

KTH doctoral recruitment is often connected to advertised doctoral positions and funded projects. The outreach email should not simply ask whether she can supervise a self-defined topic. It should ask whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities connected to her current projects.

### Risk 2｜The applicant’s “intergenerational” theme may be too broad if foregrounded too strongly

Sanna Kuoppamäki’s current projects focus more on older adults, care workers, AI, robots, wearables, and digital health than on intergenerational community design. The intergenerational angle should be presented as part of a broader stakeholder ecology rather than as the central claim in the first email.

### Risk 3｜Her work is more HCI / digital health than traditional industrial design

The applicant should avoid sounding like a product-form designer. Industrial design should be translated into human-centred interaction, ergonomics, inclusive design, smart product/service systems, and participatory design research.
