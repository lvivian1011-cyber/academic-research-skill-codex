# Outreach Strategy｜Sanna Kuoppamäki, KTH

## 1. Core Strategy

Use a **70/30 strategy**:

```text
70%: Sanna Kuoppamäki’s current projects and research agenda
30%: applicant’s own PhD direction and background
```

The strongest approach is not to present a fully independent proposal first. Instead, the applicant should position her research interest as a natural extension of the supervisor’s current work on ageing, AI, HCI, digital health, and care/welfare technologies.

## 2. Why This Strategy Fits KTH

For KTH and similar Nordic systems, PhD recruitment is often connected to advertised doctoral positions, project funding, and research-group needs. Therefore, the first email should not only ask whether the professor can supervise. It should ask whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities or project-related openings where the applicant’s background could be relevant.

## 3. What to Emphasise

Emphasise:

- ageing and older adults;
- participatory and inclusive design;
- responsible AI in care/community contexts;
- conversational AI, welfare technology, and digital health;
- older adults’ wellbeing and social connection;
- applicant’s background in industrial design, ergonomics, design psychology, and smart product design.

## 4. What Not to Over-emphasise First

Use carefully:

- intergenerational digital community services;
- broad social innovation language;
- design anthropology as the main label;
- a completely fixed proposal title.

These are still important, but they should be translated into her HCI/digital health/ageing-technology vocabulary.

## 5. Recommended Email Structure

### Paragraph 1｜Who I am and why I am writing

Introduce the applicant as an industrial design lecturer with research and teaching experience in design psychology, ergonomics, smart product design, and community-oriented design.

### Paragraph 2｜Why her work

Mention 1–2 project lines, ideally:

- CareQuAI;
- ROBOWELL;
- LugnAI;
- Age-Wear;
- Kitchen Companion.

Do not list every project in detail. Select the projects that best match the final research angle.

### Paragraph 3｜Proposed research alignment

Use a flexible sentence such as:

> I am developing a PhD direction around the participatory and inclusive design of AI-enabled care and community support technologies for older adults’ wellbeing.

Then connect this to stakeholder participation, social acceptability, responsible AI, and everyday care/community contexts.

### Paragraph 4｜Ask for opportunity, not only supervision

Recommended wording:

> I would be grateful to know whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities in your group or related projects, and whether my background could be relevant to your current research agenda.

## 6. Best Email Angle

The strongest first-email angle is:

```text
CareQuAI + ROBOWELL + applicant’s participatory/inclusive design background
```

Secondary supporting angle:

```text
LugnAI / Age-Wear + applicant’s industrial design, ergonomics, design psychology, and smart product design background
```

## 7. Recommended Attachments

Attach only:

1. CV;
2. a 1-page research interest summary.

Do not attach a long full proposal in the first email unless specifically requested.

## 8. Possible One-line Research Identity for This Supervisor

> I aim to investigate how older adults, care professionals, family members, community actors, and technology designers can participate in shaping responsible AI-enabled care and community support technologies that are socially acceptable, inclusive, and meaningful in everyday life.

Before sending the email, prepare:

- a short targeted email of 3–4 paragraphs;
- a 1-page research interest summary;
