# Papers / Project Lines to Read｜Sanna Kuoppamäki

## Reading Priority

The purpose of reading her work is not only to summarize papers, but to identify the best email entry point and possible PhD positioning.

## Priority 1｜CareQuAI

**Why read first**: strongest connection to responsible AI, care service systems, care workers, equality, inclusion, and long-term care.

**What to extract**:

- how AI-driven platform care is defined;
- how care workers and older adults are positioned;
- whether participation/co-design appears as a method or principle;
- what social consequences of AI are emphasised;
- how this could connect to community-based wellbeing and inclusive service design.

**Possible proposal use**:

- responsible AI in ageing societies;
- participatory design of AI-enabled care services;
- stakeholder participation and social acceptability.

## Priority 2｜ROBOWELL

- co-design of conversational agents / robots with older adults and stakeholders;
