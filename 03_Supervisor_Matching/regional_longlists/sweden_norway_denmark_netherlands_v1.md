## How to Use This Longlist

- A = first-round tailored email / high-value target.
- A- = strong target, but needs clearer vacancy, funding, or supervision route.
- B+ = second-round target or possible co-supervisor.
- B = keep in longlist; contact only with a specific project/vacancy.
- C = use as reference only; do not contact now unless a precise opportunity appears.

Fit score scale:

- 5.0 = excellent fit with ageing + digital/community/care + participatory/design methods.
- 4.5 = very strong fit with one small mismatch.
- 4.0 = strong secondary fit.
- 3.5 = partial fit; useful for method/theory/co-supervision.
- 3.0 = weak-to-medium fit.

# Email Strategy by Country

## Sweden

Sweden is strongly vacancy-based. Cold email should ask about upcoming doctoral positions or whether a planned funded project may open a PhD role. Do not write as if a professor can simply admit you independently.

Recommended phrasing:

> I am writing to ask whether there may be upcoming doctoral opportunities in your group or related projects where my background in industrial design, participatory design and ageing-related community services could be relevant.

## Denmark

Denmark has a mix of annual/open calls and project-specific PhD positions. ITU is especially important because the Digital Design / Welfare Technology ecosystem is very close to Vivi's direction.

Recommended phrasing:

> I am preparing for the next PhD call and would be grateful to know whether my research direction might fit your current work on welfare technology, ageing and participatory design.

## Norway

Norway is usually position/vacancy-based through PhD Research Fellow roles. Email should be project-focused and concise.

Recommended phrasing:

> I am following PhD research fellow opportunities in Norway and would like to ask whether my background could fit any upcoming projects on welfare technology, ageing and participatory design.

## Netherlands

The Netherlands is primarily employed PhD vacancy-based, but external scholarship/self-funded PhD routes can exist depending on the university. Cold email should be careful: ask whether they recommend applying to vacancies, external funding routes, or potential co-supervision.

Recommended phrasing:

> I understand that many Dutch PhD positions are vacancy-based. I would be grateful for your advice on whether my research direction could fit your group, and whether there may be suitable vacancies or externally funded PhD routes to watch.
