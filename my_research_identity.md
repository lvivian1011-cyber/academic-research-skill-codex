
# My Research Identity

## Background
I am a lecturer in Industrial Design with around 8 years of teaching and interdisciplinary project experience. My work connects industrial design, design psychology, ergonomics, smart product design, service design, and community-oriented design.

## Research Interests
My PhD interests include:
- ageing and intergenerational communities
- service design and participatory design
- design anthropology and ethnographic methods
- responsible AI in community services
- inclusive and age-friendly digital systems

## Current Research Direction
My tentative direction is Intergenerational Digital Community Services. I am interested in how co-design, ethnography, and situated prototyping can support older adults, children, families, and community stakeholders in creating more inclusive community services.

## Writing Preference
I prefer writing that is:
- sincere but not exaggerated
- academically grounded but not over-fixed
- open-ended enough for PhD application
- connected to supervisors' work without sounding like I am forcing a perfect match
